# Sources and use / 来源与使用

The class-created files in this folder are two short Markdown notes, two versions of a formula reference, a schematic SVG, a CSV, a JSON file, and two one-page PDF examples. `M-02` is fictional. The PDF examples contain the same key phrase; one stores page text, while the other embeds a raster image without a text layer. They are not copies of research papers. The `missing-web/` subfolder is a third-party reading excerpt with its own [source, license, and change note](missing-web/SOURCE.md).

本文件夹的两份 Markdown 笔记、中英文公式速查、SVG 示意图、CSV、JSON 和两份单页 PDF 均为 2026 年预习课二自编样例。`M-02` 是虚构样品。两份 PDF 含有相同的关键短语：一份存有页面文字，另一份嵌入位图，没有文字层。它们不是科研论文的副本。`missing-web/` 是第三方阅读选段，其[来源、许可和改动说明](missing-web/SOURCE.md)单独记录。

The bibliographic record in `paper-record.en.md` and `paper-record.zh.md` names a real publication and links to its DOI and original publisher page. It includes only identifying metadata. The `markdown-guide/` folder contains a reused Markdown Guide cheat sheet and its course translation; see its [source and changes](markdown-guide/SOURCE.md). Other external teaching references point to their original pages. No third-party research paper or paper image is redistributed here.

`paper-record.en.md` 和 `paper-record.zh.md` 记录真实文献的题名、作者、年份、DOI 与出版方原始页面，仅含识别文献所需的元数据。`markdown-guide/` 收录复用的 Markdown Guide 语法速查及课程译文，详见其[来源与改动](markdown-guide/SOURCE.md)。其余外部教学参考资料链接到原始页面；这里没有重新分发第三方科研论文的正文或图片。
